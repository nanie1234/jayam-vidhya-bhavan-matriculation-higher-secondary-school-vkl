A school with a purpose

Education that builds knowledge, values and confidence.

Jayam Vidhya Bhavan Matric Higher Secondary School is an English-medium school run by Sindhu Educational Trust, a registered body founded in 2001. The school is headed by the Correspondent.

Our approach recognises the diverse skills, abilities and prior knowledge of pupils. We encourage individual learning styles and aim to nurture respectful, responsible and passionate citizens.

“Strive to succeed.” — Our school motto

We believe success is not a destination, but an ever-going journey of learning and achievement.
